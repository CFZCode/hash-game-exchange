## Casino

`/casino` and `/casino/:tab?` — the original's own route, and where the home page's CASINO category card points.

**Its chunk, its filter chunk and its stylesheet are all on `hash.game` and the page was built without them.** `index-DTOmRFSX.js` (39.6 KB), `Filter-CB8e5Rdj.js` (1.8 KB) and `index-CflFQ6HO.css` (422 B) are fetchable under the hashes `Enter-MsmOuq5R.js` already lists for the route, along with `IconBaccarat-BVqEdz90.js` and the other four tab icons the page currently draws from the greyscale sprite because "their chunks were never saved". The page predates that finding and was transcribed from a desktop screenshot instead, so **check the chunk before measuring anything here.**

It is mostly composition — six tiny Solid templates over shared components — but composition is exactly what a screenshot cannot give you, and the first audit against it found two divergences that were both invisible at the captured width:

- **The Poker tab's panels are a 1 / 2 / 3-column grid of `w-full` cards** (`grid w-full grid-cols-1 gap-x-3 gap-y-6 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3`), not the `flex-wrap` row of `w-[24.75rem] flex-none` cards the measurements produced. Every measured value was right — 396px panels, `p-4`, a 16px radius, a 12px gap, three tiles across, `size-8` arrows — and the mechanism was wrong, so the two agree at the desktop width the capture was taken at and nowhere else: one panel per row across the whole 640–1023 band where the original fits two. `h-fit` on the panel is the chunk's and is not a no-op either, since a grid item stretches by default; the original lets an empty panel be short. `border-input` is the chunk's against a measured `border-third`, which is `#e4eaf019` — an alpha token that composites to roughly the same colour over layer2.
- **The tab strip carries `px-4` on the phone arm**, and without it the tabs were the one block on the page not inset. The chunk writes the bar as `S.mobile ? "sticky left-0 top-14 z-10 -mx-4 bg-layer2 px-4 py-2" : "sticky left-0 top-15 z-10 w-full bg-layer2 py-2"` — the mobile arm cancels the page gutter so the opaque bar reaches the viewport edges, then puts the 16px back inside it. This build's column has no inset below `sm` and each block adds its own `px-4 sm:px-0`, so the `-mx-4` half was already true structurally and only the padding was missing. Note the `top-14` / `top-15` pair is this project's `--header-h` exactly, which settles that `S.mobile` is the **1024** line here and not the 640 one.

**`.grid-editor` is the whole of the page's stylesheet** and it is the `--grid-column` machinery from `index.css` with the media queries left off — a flat three columns at every width. That is what fills a Poker panel, and it is why three tiles across in a panel is a constant rather than a breakpoint.

**Two departures on the tab strip, both requested.** The chevrons are gone and the strip is dragged instead — `ScrollTabs` gained an `arrows` prop for it, defaulting to `true` so Explore's strip keeps the site's own control, and the casino strip passes `false`. **Grab-to-scroll is mouse-only**, and that is not a shortcut: a touch pointer already scrolls the strip natively with momentum and rubber-banding that a `pointermove` handler cannot match, so taking it over would be strictly worse on the devices the page is being tuned for. The part of it that is not optional is the click swallower — every child is a link, so a drag ending over a tab would navigate on release; `onClickCapture` is the only place to stop that without each call site knowing. Note `scroll-smooth` has to come off for the duration of a drag (it applies to programmatic `scrollLeft` writes too, so the strip would lag the pointer and glide on after release) and go back on afterwards, so the arrows' and the centring effect's own smooth scrolls are untouched.

The drag itself now lives in **`hooks/useDragScroll.js`**, lifted the day the Poker tab's panels became its second consumer — the same rule `Select`, `GameTile`, `PopCenter` and `ShowBlocked` moved under. It returns the props to spread on a scroller plus its class fragment, and it is this build's rather than the original's: the site scrolls its strips with the chevrons and nothing else.

**The Poker panels' arrows show at EVERY width, and that is the chunk's own split.** `Controller` gained `hideOnMobile`, defaulting to true because that is the HOME row's gate — `ot` renders it only when `!m.mobile`, since a home row is swipeable and the arrows are a pointer affordance. The chunk wires the panel's controller separately (`x1`, with a `smoothListSelector`) and carries no such test, which is what the reference capture of Hold'em and Teen Patti shows.

**The strip's phone margins are tighter than its desktop ones**, also requested, and every pair is written `phone sm:desktop` so nothing reaches 640 and up. Above the bar goes to `mt-0`, leaving its own `py-2` as the whole gap from the search field — 16px to 8. Below, `mb-5` becomes `mb-1`, and **that one has a floor that is not ours**: the gap is max(this margin, the first `SectionHeader`'s `mt-3`), because adjacent margins collapse to the larger. So the phone arm lands on the header's 12px rather than its own 4, the total goes 28px to 20, and anything under `mb-3` there changes nothing at all. Tighter than that means editing `SectionHeader`, which is also the home page's.

## The list pages

`/section/:section`, `/gamelist/:type`, `/tagname/:tag` and `/game-related` — the original's own four paths, and where every **All** pill goes. The home page's six rows point at three of them (`/gamelist/brand`, `/section/hot_games`, `/section/exclusive`, `/gamelist/slots`, `/gamelist/live`, `/tagname/bingo?label=All%20Bingo%20Games`), the casino lobby's Poker and Feature Buy-in rows at a fourth and fifth `/tagname/…`, and the Recommended Games row under `/favorite` and `/recent` at `/section/hot_games?label=Recommended`. **Every one of those links was already written** — they are the feed's and the chunks' own hrefs — and every one landed on the catch-all, which renders Home. So this is four pages built rather than a link rewired.

Four chunks off `hash.game/assets/`, none in the mirror and all tiny: `index-BSVarTKc.js` (3.1 KB), `index-BM71IaL0.js` (6.5 KB), `index-BaCzwozn.js` (1.6 KB) and `index-BtGOZZxv.js` (1.1 KB). Between them they are one shape — `<PageLayout title>` over `<PcNav title/>` and a grid — which is why one component serves all four here, the way `pages/GameCollection` serves `/favorite` and `/recent`. `PcNav` is the shared one those two brought in; these are the other two of the four routes that mount it.

**THE TITLE IS A FALLBACK CHAIN, NOT A MAP**: `searchParams.label ?? MAP[key] ?? key`. A link may carry its own label — which is what the Bingo row and the Recommended Games row both rely on — and an unknown key prints itself rather than 404ing. Both maps are the chunks' own and verbatim: 21 section titles out of `W` in `index-BSVarTKc.js` and 16 tag titles out of `f` in `index-BaCzwozn.js`. **`__ENV_SITE__` EXPANDS TO "BC", NOT "BC.GAME"** — `license.site` is the literal `"BC"` in `index-BsFv7ByC.js`, where `license.host` is `"bc.game"` — so the two titles carrying it are "BC Originals" and "BC Exclusive", which is what the sidebar row, the casino tab and the home row each call them too. The Buy Crypto disclaimer's "BC.GAME" is a different token, `__ENV_HOST__`, which is `host.toUpperCase()`; conflating the two is what made these read "BC.GAME Originals" for one revision.

**THE HOME PAGE'S BC ORIGINALS ROW IS THE WHOLE SELECTION NOW — 73 games rather than the page of ten the live site renders, and it is a requested departure.** The feed behind it turned out to be reachable: `POST /api/edge/game/home/recommend/selection/` with `{selectionName: "originals", page: 1, pageSize: 100}` answers unauthenticated on hash.game and returns `total: 73` in one page, artwork URLs and live `onlineUsers` included — a recommendation feed is a catalogue, which is the `game/support` split again. **The mirror's own capture is the BATCH call beside it** (`.../batch/selections/`), which returns page 1 of every selection at once, so the ten games each home row fixture holds are a PAGE and not a list; the same trick will lengthen any of the other five. Two visible consequences on this row: it opens on **Crash**, which the live page's ten-tile slice leaves out and which the whole selection cannot, and Fast Crash sits where the feed puts it rather than being lifted to tenth. Sixty-nine of the covers were already local; the four that were not were pulled from the response's own `iconThreeFour` URLs **through the live DOM's query** (`?_v=4,dpr=1.25,width=200`, the 250x333 variant at ~44KB) rather than bare, which serves a 960x1280 master ten times the size. Note the row and its own All page **do not hold the same list and neither is wrong**: `/gamelist/brand` is the casino BC Originals TAB's 63 games, transcribed off that tab, while this is the `originals` recommendation selection.

**WHICH GAMES A LIST SHOWS IS THIS BUILD'S, and it is the one part of these pages that is.** On the original each list is a `selection/sub-list/` POST paging server-side through thousands of games; here a route resolves to one of `pages/Casino/catalogue.js`'s sets — the same lists the casino tabs draw, most of them transcribed off the live tabs rather than filtered out of the 118-game capture. So `/gamelist/brand` is 63 games, `/section/hot_games` 120, `/gamelist/slots` 97. Two destinations have no casino set and are not empty either, because the live site has no tab for them: BC Exclusive and Bingo are home ROWS, and each resolves to its own home fixture, so a row and its All page cannot disagree. **An unmapped key resolves to nothing and renders the site's own empty state**, which is the original's `default: return []` — Providers, Themes and the five never-captured sections (Top Picks, Pachinko, Fishing, Trending, Spin To Win) all take it, and inventing a set for a section this project has never seen would be worse.

**The All pill became a router `<Link>` with it, and that is a fidelity fix rather than a nicety.** `ot` renders it as `rh`, the router's own `<A>`, which emits a real `<a href>` and then intercepts the click — `preventDefault` plus a queued `navigate()`, which is exactly what React Router's `<Link>` does. It was a bare `<a>` here for as long as every destination fell through to the catch-all, where a reload and a client-side navigation are indistinguishable; they are not, now that these pages exist. `GameSection`'s View All tile (`yl`) took the same change.

**`py-4` BELONGS TO THE GRID'S WRAPPER, NOT THE PAGE ROOT.** All four chunks pass `class="py-4"` to the list component, which sits BELOW `PcNav`, so nothing on the original pads above the title — putting it on the root opened an extra 16px over the heading. With it where it belongs the heading's top edge is the column's own padding-top, which is where `/favorite` puts it and where that page's stack was measured against the capture.

Not built: both filter rows the chunks carry — Sort By, the All Providers multi-select with its own search, and Show Blocked, on `/gamelist/slots` and `/gamelist/live` — which need `sortBy` and `providerNames` to reach a server that answers; and `/game-related`'s own list, which asks `recommended_games` with a live session's `relatedGameIds` and so renders its heading over the empty state, the same call `RecommendList` documents.

## The home page's Continue Playing / Favorites block

`src/pages/Home/RecentFavorites/` — the signed-in home page's shortcut to those two pages, built after the star on the game page started producing rows for one of them. Source is `wu` → `vu` → `bu` in `Enter-MsmOuq5R.js`, with the gate in `yn` and the read in `_u`; nothing here is off a screenshot.

**IT IS ONE SECTION WITH TABS, NOT TWO SECTIONS, and that is the thing worth getting right.** "A Favorites section is added to the home page" reads naturally as a second `GameSection` beside BC Originals, and an account with only favourites renders identically either way — so the mistake would survive any screenshot taken to check it. `bu` is one `hu` whose **title is a row of tab buttons** (`fu` = `<div class="flex items-center">` over `Au`) and whose `link` and `data` both follow the active tab. An account with a play history *and* a favourite gets one block it flips between, and the **All** pill retargets between `/recent` and `/favorite` as it does. Two stacked sections would put two headers and two strips on the page and double its height for exactly the account the feature is for.

**Where it sits: between the hero and Recent Big Wins.** `Dn` is `[wu, Iu, Ao]` — this block, Game of the Week (`Iu`, Korea-only, not built here), then Recent Big Wins — and `ng()` inserts the whole of it as the **first** child of the page body, ahead of the category cards and every ordered game row. So the signed-in order is hero, this, big wins, categories, BC Originals.

**Why the section appears the moment you star something, and why nothing watches the star.** `yn` is a three-state gate, not a truthiness check:

```js
function yn(e, n, t, o) {                    // (read, wasShown, setShown, tag)
  return () => { const i = e();
    if (i !== void 0) { const s = i.length > 0; return t(s),
      s ? i.map(l => ({ ...l, autoTag: o })) : void 0 }   // EMPTY becomes undefined
    return n() ? [] : void 0 } }                          // pending: [] if shown before
```

and `vu` then `.filter(g => g.data !== void 0)`. Rows → the tab renders. **Empty → `undefined` → the tab is dropped entirely.** Pending → `[]` if this visitor has seen that tab before, else `undefined`. So the block is rendered from the read on every home visit and an empty favourites list is indistinguishable from no favourites feature at all: star a game, the next read returns a row, the tab exists; un-star the last one and it is gone. **There is no "has favourites" flag anywhere and adding one would be this build's invention.**

The third arm is the reason for the two persisted booleans (`continue_playing_show`, `favorite_show`, the original's own keys, through this build's `createPersistedStore`). `[]` is `GameSection`'s loading state — eight skeleton tiles — so a returning visitor who had favourites last time gets a placeholder strip of the right height while the read is in flight and the page does not jump when it lands, while a visitor who never had one gets nothing rather than a skeleton that resolves to absent. Same distinction `docs/data-layer.md` draws about a declared `initialValue`: **the remembered shape is not a spinner.**

**One call there, two here.** `_u` POSTs `/game/home/recent-and-like/` once and reads `[0]` as history and `[1]` as favourites; this platform has no combined route, so `getRecentAndFavorites` in `services/collection.js` composes the two the pages already use and keeps the tuple, which is what `vu` is written against. `Promise.all` rather than a sequence — the block is gated on both, and resolving them one at a time would show the first tab alone and then slide it sideways. A failed read falls back to `[]` on each arm, which the gate turns into an absent tab: **a home page missing one strip beats an error where a strip used to be.** `pageSize` is `_u`'s own **10**, not the pages' 40 — the row scrolls and hands off to the View All tile, so asking for forty would fetch four times what it can show. `getRecentGames` gained the `limit` option `getFavoriteGames` already had so the pair reads with one argument; both pages pass nothing and keep 40.

**Padding is `de`'s, not `hu`'s, deliberately.** The original wraps the block in `qd` = `<div class="px-4 sm:px-0">` because `hu` is a second, thinner section component — a bare `<div class="mb-4">` around `ot` + `go` — whose header does not carry that inset. This build has one section component, `GameSection` (the port of `de`), which already puts `px-4 sm:px-0` on its own header and leaves the strip full-bleed to `--grid-padding`. Wrapping it again would double the inset below `sm` and pull the strip off the scroll edge every other home row sits on.

**Only one tab can currently appear, and that is gap 2 rather than a bug here.** `GET /casino/games/recently-played` answers `[]` for every account on this delivery — only the GIS launch path writes it and GIS is unsynced — so the history arm gates to `undefined` and Continue Playing never renders. Verified: `verify-favorites-home.mjs` asserts the Favorites tab appears **alone**, which is the correct rendering until that write exists and not a placeholder for it.

**Verified against the running platform**, ten assertions in `verify-favorites-home.mjs`: home renders signed in with no Favorites tab, two games starred through the real routes, the tab then present with the starred game named in the strip, the page still intact either side, recently-played still empty so no Continue Playing tab, and the tab gone again after un-starring. The rows are removed afterwards and the two `_show` keys cleared between renders — a leftover `favorite_show` is exactly what would make an absent section render a skeleton, which is the one way this could pass while being wrong.

### Three corrections to the block, from a first look at it running

**The cards drew the brand placeholder instead of their covers.** `toFavouriteGame` sets `icon: undefined` deliberately — its comment says that means "use this build's own artwork, which is what the tile does with `assetExact(slug)`" — and **the tile did not do that**. `GameTile`'s `src` was `image || placeholderArt`, so a row this build resolved locally skipped straight past its own art to the grey BC card. Now `image || assetExact(slug) || placeholderArt`. `assetExact` and not `assetFor`: no fuzzy rule, so a game with genuinely no file still reads as missing rather than silently borrowing another game's cover. Worth noting the failure mode — a comment asserting what a *different* file does is not a check, and this one had been wrong since the day it was written.

**The cards were missing their corner badge, and that was a piece of `yn` left on the floor.** The gate was ported and the map beside it was not:

```js
s ? i.map(l => z(R({}, l), { autoTag: o })) : void 0     // o is "history" | "favorite"
```

`autoTag` is not bookkeeping — it is what puts the badge on the card. The tile's own markup is

```js
r(D, { when: !e.data.isRecItem, children: [
  r(D, { when: e.data.autoTag === "history",  children: r(Gr, { class: "absolute start-1.5 top-1.5 size-5 fill-alw_white" }) }),
  r(D, { when: e.data.autoTag === "favorite", children: r(Fr, { class: "absolute start-1.5 top-1.5 size-5 fill-alw_white" }) })
]})
```

so a star for a favourite and a clock for a played game, top-left, 20px, white. `Fr` is `Mr` and `Gr` is `Hr`, and **each is ONE path holding a glyph subpath and a disc subpath** — the default `nonzero` fill rule is what makes the glyph read against the disc, so they must not be split into two paths and must not be given a `fill-rule` or a `fill` of their own. Both transcribed whole into `GameTile`. The badge is a LABEL, not a control: it says why this card is in this strip, so the same game elsewhere on the page wears nothing, and `!isRecItem` is the original's own guard that a recommendation is never tagged.

**Starring a game deleted the Recommended Games row from `/favorite`.** `RecommendList` returned `null` whenever its `list` was non-empty, because the related arm (`recommended_games` + `relatedGameIds`) is unbuilt and printing `hot_games` under its name would have been one selection's games under the other's. That reasoning was written when both feeds answered 6002, so `list` was *always* empty, the branch was unreachable and the guard was theory. Favourites returning rows made it live — and its effect was that the emptier the page, the more it showed.

That is backwards, and it is not the original either: `h` renders `GameRecommend` when the list is full and `g` when it is empty, and **both print "Recommended Games"**. The row is unconditional there; only the feed changes. So the guard is gone and the fallback renders in both arms. **The departure is real:** on a page with favourites the original shows games related to them and this shows `hot_games`. It is not a mislabelling — the heading is the one the original prints either way — and the alternative on the table was never "the related feed" but "no row at all", which is further from the original rather than closer. ### The block is on the casino lobby too, and the strip is padded

**`/casino` mounts the same component, and the chunk says so by name.** `index-DTOmRFSX.js` — off `hash.game/assets/`, not in the mirror — opens with `import{ … ao as H1 … }from"./Enter-MsmOuq5R.js"`, and `ao` is `wu` in Enter's export map: the Continue Playing / Favorites wrapper. Its placement is the lobby's first child, ahead of the ordered rows:


the same position `ng()` gives it on home. **LOBBY ONLY** — it sits in the section list, so Slots, Poker, Themes and Providers never show it. Nothing else was needed: the component already carries its own login and empty-list gates, so a visitor with neither favourites nor history gets nothing on either page.

**The strip is PADDED, and that is why the original's Favorites tab shows games you never starred.** `recent-and-like` does not return only your own rows: both of its lists are padded out to the page size with recommendations flagged `isRecItem: true`. That flag is the entire reason `GameTile`'s badge is gated on `!isRecItem` — so the tab reads as *your* games wearing a star among suggestions that do not. The asymmetry already recorded at the top of `services/collection.js` is the other half of it: the history PAGE applies `list.filter(i => i?.isRecItem === false)` because printing padding as "games you played" would be a lie, while padding a favourites strip is only a suggestion and is left in.

`pad()` in `services/collection.js` reproduces it. **The source of the padding is the departure, and it is the one `RecommendList` already carries:** bc.game pads from a recommendation engine keyed to the account, and this platform has none — `casino/games/recommended`, `/related`, `/popular` and `/hot` all answer `404`. So the filler is `hot_games`. Structure identical — same flag, same badge suppression, same cap of ten; only the choosing is not personalised.

Two details that are not obvious and are load-bearing. `already` de-duplicates the filler against the starred rows, which the server does for free and a local pad does not. And **an empty list is never padded**: `yn` reads empty as "drop this tab entirely", so padding it would resurrect a tab the visitor has not earned — the one place this must not follow the shape of a padded response.

**`lg:pb-8` on the page root is the gap above the footer, and it is this build's.** There was nothing to transcribe: the chunk renders `s(M, { title, children: [PcNav, grid, RecommendList] })` and `M` is export `p` of index-BsFv7ByC.js — `w3`, which is `return t.children` behind a metadata write, contributing no layout at all. Without a value of our own the Recommended Games strip sat flush against `.footer-bg` and its 1rem top margin. `lg:` and no other arm because **the page arm only renders from `lg` up** — below that the route is a dialog, which has no footer to crowd. 2rem is what the home page gives its own last row, and this page ends on the same component, so the two now end alike. Requested rather than measured, the same footing as the casino page's `pb-0 lg:pb-2`.

`verify-favorites-home.mjs` covers all of it — 23 assertions, including that `/casino` shows the tab and `/casino/slots` does not, that the padded list is ten rows of which eight carry `isRecItem`, and that **only two badges render for ten cards**.

### Recent Big Wins is excluded from the scroll reveal

## Opening a game

`/game/:gameUnique`'s play area — how hash.game launches a game, what this build does, and why the two differ where they do. Read with `pages/Game/index.jsx` and `services/game.js`, whose notes cite each piece.

### How the original does it

The game page is in the federated `games` remote (rung 3 of the recovery ladder): `hash.game/modules/games/remoteEntry.js` → `manifest-kUovHkMN.js` (the deploy has since moved to `manifest-DU8b-4T-.js`; the old hashes still answer) → `GameLayout` = `Layout-DI8MpYIQ.js`. A third-party slot loads the `slots` module `index-CxQFC9RQ.js` over `SlotsGameLayout-D-fxtFdC.js` and `createSlotsEnter-B77pk5hp.js`; a score game and BC Poker take other modules. Fetched with a browser UA into the session scratchpad, not the repo.

- **Nothing launches on arrival.** The standard slot's policy is `{id: "standard", hasCurrencyOverlay: true, autoLaunch: "never", singleUseSrc: true}`. The frame shows the game's `iconThreeFour` cover (`blur-[1px]`) under an overlay card, and the launch runs from that card's button.
- **The overlay card** (`ve` in the slots module): `absolute inset-0 flex justify-center items-center z-20 bg-black/60` over a `w-[30rem] bg-layer3 p-8 rounded-xl` card.
  - Signed out: "The selected currency will be displayed in <c>, and if you change currency while playing, the game will refresh and restart." and a brand **Sign in** (`navigate("/login/signin")`), plus a second **Free Play** when the game supports demo. There is no Sign Up on this card at all, so a B2B site needs nothing removed here.
  - Signed in: "Play with balance in" and a wallet `Select` (`We`), a status line (`qe`: `auto`, `realmoneyplay`, `novaluecurrency`, `gamenobonus`, `unwhitelistcurrency` — the last reads "<c> isn't supported for this game right now.Please choose another currency to continue playing.", missing space included), and a brand `w-64` **Play With Deposit Balance** (or **Deposit Now** when the balance is empty), plus **Free Play** with demo.
- **Real vs demo.** `onPlay({isDemo})`: real POSTs `/platform-slots/launch/` with `{gameUnique, currency, device: 2, demo: false, areaCode, showCurrency, bonus, fsId}` and frames `response.url`; demo POSTs `/platform-slots/preload/` (works signed out). The footer's **Demo / Real Play** switch (`StandardFreePlaySwitch-CMNLQAez.js`, `flex center h-10 rounded-lg bg-layer2 p-1`) returns early unless `supportDemo` — so a game without demo shows neither the switch nor any Free Play button.
- **The frame** (`at` in SlotsGameLayout): `w-full relative overflow-hidden after:pt-[56.25%] game-iframe-wrap` — 16:9 across the column (1168×657 at 1440) — holding `<iframe class="absolute left-0 top-0 size-full border-none game-iframe" allow="autoplay;geolocation;camera;microphone;clipboard-read;clipboard-write" allowfullscreen>`. **No `sandbox`.** A URL is single-use (`singleUseSrc`): a repeated one is refused and the launcher resets. There is no live-players panel on a slot page; that panel is the BC Originals client's.
- **Toolbar** (Logs chunk `Ye`): Full Screen (`q`: `requestFullscreen` on `iframe.game-iframe`, else the toast "Please start the game before entering fullscreen."), the star with its count, live stats, the floating-window button, the Demo/Real switch; the settings menu holds Like, Share and **Movie Mode** (theatre: `useMovieModeLayout` widens `#game-full-container` to 100% and the layout to 1631px, or 16:9 of the viewport height above 2053px).
- **Phone** (`ic` in Layout): the same 16:9 card; under it, signed out, "Deposit now X% cash back" and **Sign in**; signed in, the balance and **Zoom & Play**, which sets the frame `fixed inset-0 z-[9999]` with a close/minimise bar.
- **Failure.** A launch error is a toast with the API's message ("Failed to launch game", "Game not available"). A game the detail read marks `DISABLED_PLAYING` pushes `re` from `GamePageData-B2XkLpDN.js`: the popup card titled **Under Maintenance**, a grey wrench (`#B1B9B9`, 48 viewBox at `w-10 h-10`), "This game is currently unavailable. Please try other games." and a full-width brand **OK** that only pops it — the player stays on the page. - **RTP / provider info** sits under the frame (`Ro`): title, "By <provider>", Live RTP / Real RTP (%) / Volatility / Max Win from `slotsInfo`, favourite and share counts.

### What this build does

- **Play is a press.** No launch on mount (it used to launch on every visit, opening a provider session and writing Recently Played for players who were only looking). The card is the original's, signed out and signed in; Sign in goes to `/login/signin?redirect=/game/<slug>`.
- **The bodies.** v2 (catalogue) `{gameUid: <catalogue uuid = js_games.game_uid>, currencyCode, language}`; v1 (in-house) the same plus `homeUrl` (this page). Both validators are `.strict()` — v2 refuses `homeUrl` and `demo` with a 422.
- **The currency is the selected wallet** (`session.setting.currencyName`, `INRFIAT` → `INR`), not a constant. v2 is INR-only upstream: with SmartBet's keys the identical body answered 201 for INR (BGaming `bgaming-asia.com`, Pragmatic `…/gs2c/playGame.do`, Evolution `…evo-games.com/entry`) and `502 JSGAMES_UPSTREAM_REJECTED` (provider HTTP 400) for USDT. A wallet the route cannot settle gets the original's `unwhitelistcurrency` line with Play disabled, and the card's picker lists only the wallets that can — picking writes the header's own selection. Agent players hold INR and launch like anyone else; casino is not a promotion and nothing gates it for them.
- **No Free Play / Demo.** Neither js-games route has a demo launch, so `supportDemo` is false everywhere and — by the original's own rule — the switch and both Free Play buttons are absent.
- **Third-party games take the slot layout**: 16:9 frame from `lg` up, no players panel (`.game-page.is-slots`). Below `lg` the frame keeps room for the card, and a running game goes fixed full-screen with a close bar (Zoom & Play); closing ends the game.
- **The iframe** carries the original's `allow` list and `allowFullScreen`, no `sandbox`, the URL unchanged. It is dropped during render when the route moves to another game or the player signs out.
- **Every failure opens Under Maintenance** — 503 `*_NOT_CONFIGURED`, `JSGAMES_UPSTREAM_*`, 403 `NOT_ENTITLED` (owner flag), 422, a network failure, a success with no URL, the fixture build — and a slug the catalogue does not hold opens it on arrival, once. OK and the cross both only close it. The code goes to the console, not the screen.
- **Full Screen is live** (the original's `q`, toast included). Settings (Like/Share/Movie Mode) and the swap button stay presentational: nothing backs Like/Share, and Movie Mode resizes a page container this layout does not have.

### Backend

`jsGames.service.js#upstream` reported a refusal sent as HTTP 200 + `{success:false}` as `UPSTREAM_FAILED` ("could not be reached"); it is `UPSTREAM_REJECTED` now, with the provider's code in `details.upstreamCode`. `__tests__/jsGamesLaunch.test.js` proves both launch requests against a mocked provider (URL, method, signature, body, real-balance credit, session row, v1 ciphertext and `home_url`) and the refusal mapping, and pins which keys each validator takes.

### What still needs keys

`JSGAMES_V2_API_KEY` / `JSGAMES_V2_API_SECRET` for every catalogue game (the base URL is set); `JSGAMES_V1_AGENCY_UID` and a 32-byte `JSGAMES_V1_AES_KEY` for the in-house games, which this site also has switched off with the owner's `originals` flag (403 `NOT_ENTITLED`). Then `./run.sh restart casino`. For real money to settle, the provider also needs `JSGAMES_CALLBACK_URL` = `<public origin>/api/v1/casino/js-games/v2/bet-callback` reachable from the internet.

that call it uncaptured are out of date, and the way in is worth repeating.** Its filename is not
guessable and `schedule-promotions-ncwAhfK8.js`, the obvious candidate, is the "Exclusive

Three things it settles, all of them now built:

  `title: b("Promotion")` and `class: "bonus-scope dialog-item bg-layer2 text-transparent

  built here. Both open the same popup: `title: b("Archived")`, `class: "!bg-layer1 sm:!w-[480px]"`,
  holding the page's own list with `inProgress: !1`.
- **THE TAB STRIP IS THE FEED'S, AND IT DIFFERS BY DEVICE.** The strip is built from the response's
  `categoryList`, and the request carries the device in its body:
  `post("/home/main/event-pageList/", { areaCode, env: isAPP ? "APP" : mobile ? "H5" : "WEB", level,
  vip, lang, archived, page, pageSize })`. So the phone's three tabs (All / Casino / Deposit) and the

  It answers **403** for both `env` values, probed, so the H5 list is read off a capture of the live
  phone page and the archived list is empty — the site's own empty state, as everywhere else here.

Transcribed from rendered markup, so the class lists are the site's. Three things there resist tidying: **`bonus-scope` appears twice** (outer and inner wrapper) and is not decorative — `.app-root.dark .bonus-scope` in `index-DxOqNczA.css` re-declares tokens, which is where `fourth`, `quinary` and `card_dark` come from; `@container` is in the DOM but nothing uses a `@`-variant; and the card's `-ml-2` / `-mr-1.5` deliberately pull past the card's padding.

Four tokens were added to `@theme` from that dark block. **`quinary` is `#4B5052`** — far darker than a screenshot suggests, and deliberately low-contrast against `card_dark`; do not lighten it to match a compressed capture. `card_dark` duplicates `alw_dark`'s value but stays separate because the markup asks for it by name and they diverge in the light theme. `text-xxs` is the one **inferred** value on the page — the promotions chunk was never captured, so 10px comes from the `text-[10px]` badge beside it.

The tab indicator is **measured, not computed**: these tabs are `max-w-fit` with `gap-8`, so each is its own label's width (the capture has `--tabs-width: 17px` for "All"). The auth dialog shares the CSS but divides its strip evenly and can set both vars from an index; this one writes them onto the node in a layout effect, because measuring into state is the cascading render `react-hooks/set-state-in-effect` rejects.

**Card artwork is local.** All eleven PNGs live in `src/assets/promotions/` under the CDN's own hashes, pulled from `imgxcut.com/bc_event/<hash>.png?_v=4,dpr=1.25,width=800` (the 800px variant, so the 112px slot has retina headroom). Nothing under `bc_event/` is in the saved mirror — these came from the live CDN. Keeping the hash as the filename is what lets `promotions.js`'s `image` field double as the local basename with no lookup table. Note that `curl` fails on these URLs in this environment (libcurl chokes on the comma-separated `?_v=` query); PowerShell's `Invoke-WebRequest` fetches them fine.

**Tab membership is captured**, all four tabs read from the live page. Between them they account for only four of the eleven: Casino has 100x Multiplier Challenge and Free Spins Giveaway, BC Exclusive has Knockoin, Deposit has August Madness Draw. The other seven appear under All and nowhere else — that is the page's own behaviour, so an empty `categories` means "belongs to no filter tab", not "unknown". The card's `tag` ("Slots") is a badge printed on the card and is unrelated to which tab it files under.

Two **deliberate departures** from the capture, both flagged at their call sites so they are one-word reverts: the pager sits at `pt-4` where the capture has `pt-8` (the grid above already ends on `sm:gap-y-8`, so the captured pair stacks 32px on 32px), and the card description is `font-normal` where the capture has `font-semibold`. The second is worth understanding rather than just noting: this build ships Averta at **400/600/800 only**, so any weight the original uses that we do not gets synthesised by the browser instead of swapped for a real face, and semibold body copy at 12px lands heavier here than on the live page.

## Deposit Bonus

`/deposit-offer` — the original's own path (`component: c(() => L.DepositOffer())`), and what the sidebar's "Deposit Bonus" row means; that row already carried the `deposit_offer` sprite and had nowhere to go. It is a **sibling** of the promotions routes, not a child, so `SUBMENU_ROUTES` carries a second `promotions` entry to make the group open itself there.

Unusually for this project the page has **no chunk but a real service**. `index-DcrUJi8K.js` is missing, so the markup was transcribed from rendered DOM and the dialog from screenshots — but `index-CoOXrsZl.js` **is** in the mirror, is barely minified, and names the calls: `times-deposit-bonus/list/` and `.../claim/`. Watch the sibling — `cycle-deposit-bonus/*` is the **weekly** reward, a different feature; the chunk itself spells them `getTimesDepositBonus` vs `getWeeklyDepositReward`. Both families are in `endpoints.js`.

The reset countdown is **computed, not pinned**. The page states its own rule ("All 4 chances reset on the 1st of each month at 00:00 UTC") and the captured `expiresinms`, 1788220799999, is that rule to the millisecond — so `services/bonus.js` derives end-of-month UTC and the clock keeps running instead of freezing on the capture date. `stageMatch` is `bonus + rakeback`, checked at both ends: DEPOSIT 1's 180+40 is the page's own "220% Deposit Rakeback" headline, DEPOSIT 4's 360+140 its "Up to 500% bonus".

The detail dialog is a `.pop-center` — the imperative popup shell, not a route (`popupViewDetail` sits in the same export registry as `DepositOffer`) — plus its own `bonus-terms-conditions` class, whose rules are in the missing chunk and are reconstructed in `DepositOffer.css`: `width: fit-content`, no `padding-top`, hidden `.pop-title`, unpadded `.pop-content`. Every one of those four is forced by a measurement, and the width in particular is *not* a magic number — the stage grid's `sm:min-w-[36.75rem]` plus the gaps and the `w-80` calculator add up to exactly 1000, with the 4px scrollbar making the 1004 the capture shows.

**It was built from screenshots first and rewritten from rendered markup later, and the diff between those two passes is the useful part.** Geometry and colour survived intact — widths, gaps, the 48px artwork, `border-fourth/5`, `layer3` cards, the brand green, and the fact that the header is sticky *inside* a card-height scroller. **Font sizes did not: nearly every one was too large by exactly one step.** Reading a size off a glyph's height needs a ratio that depends on which letters the string happens to contain, and even calibrated against a page whose classes are known it over-reads — the stage label measured 13.7 and is `text-xs`, "Min deposit" measured 10.9 and is `text-[0.625rem]`, both section headings measured 17–19 and are `text-base`. **Line-height pitch is the one exact method** (Tailwind's steps carry fixed line-heights, so a paragraph's baseline spacing names its class) and it was right every time. Use it; do not trust cap-height arithmetic to choose between adjacent steps.

Two things the eye got wrong that pixels settled: the scrim looks nearly transparent but samples to `rgba(16,18,18,0.8)`, exactly what `.pop-overlayer` writes inline in `index-BsFv7ByC.js`; and "RakeBack" looks like a second, duller green but samples `#24EE89`. A third: the rendered DOM ships a `.pop-title` with a close button that is **invisible** — `.pop-close` paints a `#373E3F` square and there is not one pixel of it anywhere in the capture, which is how the hidden-title-bar rule was arrived at rather than guessed.

Three things on this page are **this build's, not the capture's**, each flagged at its call site: the footer's prose/feedback band is hidden here (it is now hidden on every route but `/` — see the footer note below — which subsumes the entry this page once needed in the old `BLURB_HIDDEN_ON` prefix denylist, and it needed one because the path is not under `/promotions`), the promotions grid carries `mb-6` so the last row does not butt into the footer, and **the page scroll lock now runs on desktop**. That last one contradicts a reading of the minified source and is right anyway. `useDialogScrollLock` was ported with an `isSupportTouch` gate, taken verbatim from `index-BsFv7ByC.js`, on which a desktop never locks — but the live site plainly does: open a dialog there and the page behind loses its scrollbar and **grows into that 15px strip**. Behaviour beats the reading, so the gate is gone.

**Do not compensate for that reflow** — a rule this build has since reversed twice, on request, and the current state is that it does compensate: `:root` carries `overflow-y: scroll` so the 15px gutter is held permanently and the page no longer jumps sideways when a dialog opens. It is a **deliberate difference from the site**, which reflows, so anyone chasing pixel parity on dialog open should delete it first. A `position: fixed` body was tried as the way to keep the track drawn as well and is reverted for good: a fixed body becomes a stacking context, and every fixed overlay then resolves its z-index inside it rather than against the page — the header stayed lit above a dialog's scrim.

**THE PAGE IS NOT LOCKED BEHIND A DIALOG AT ALL ANY MORE, and that is the requested trade rather than an oversight.** The gutter rule is what started it: `body`'s overflow reaches the viewport only through CSS Overflow §3.3, which propagates it **only while the root element's own overflow is `visible` in both axes**, so declaring `overflow-y: scroll` on `:root` ended the propagation and `.lock-scroll` quietly became inert. Locking the root instead does work — and it takes the scrollbar with it, because a track is painted only for a container that has somewhere to scroll. That was tried, and the reserved 15px came back as bare page background, which reads as the scrollbar vanishing on dialog open because it is exactly that.

**The two cannot both be had, and keeping the bar was chosen.** `index.css` carries the full list at `.lock-scroll`: `overflow: hidden` on the root loses the track, on `body` is inert, a `100vh` body keeps the track but loses the thumb, and `position: fixed` on body is the only thing that holds both — reverted for an unrelated reason (a fixed body is a stacking context, so every fixed overlay resolves its z-index inside it and the header stayed lit above a dialog's scrim). So nothing carries the lock, `.lock-scroll` stays on `<body>` as a marker with a faithful hook behind it, and a rule can be hung off it the day the trade is made the other way.

**The thumb is `#4a5354` and has no token.** The original writes it as native nesting — `.app-root.dark { ::-webkit-scrollbar-thumb { background-color: #4a5354 !important } }` — and the port had reached for the nearest token, `layer6`'s `#3A4142`, which sat close enough to `layer3`/`layer4` that every in-app scroller read as an empty track. Note the pair is a DESCENDANT selector on the original too, so neither it nor the `width: 4px` beside it reaches the page's own scrollbar: what shows at the edge of the window is the browser's, on the live site as much as here.

Two literals resist tokenising: the booster badge's `#71E871` is **not** `brand_alt` (`#9FE871`) despite being one digit away, and the clock's `text-[#070F0B] dark:text-[#D5E2E5]` is the original's own pair — the dark half is `primary_alw` exactly, but the light half has no token, so the pair stays as written. The card artwork lives at `src/assets/deposit-offer/monthly.png` and both the card and the dialog head resolve it through one glob, so the fixture's `image` field doubles as the basename. It was drawn rather than fetched, because on the live site it is `/assets/monthly-BVEfL9VE.png` — bundled into the missing chunk, and bc.game's own origin times out from this environment while its CDNs do not. **That is no longer a wall: `hash.game/assets/monthly-BVEfL9VE.png` serves it, and so does every other origin-hosted asset this file calls unrecoverable** (see the mirror note near the top). The same goes for `index-DcrUJi8K.js`, the page's own missing chunk — the markup and dialog on this page were transcribed from rendered DOM and screenshots when they did not have to be. Worth re-checking against the real chunk before trusting any inferred value here, `text-xxs` in particular.

## Daily Contest

`/promotions/daily-contest` — the original's own path, loading `Contest-Uqb1GQAo.js`. Unlike `/deposit-offer` it really is under the promotions family, so the footer's prose band and the sidebar group's self-opening both already covered it.

**The page is a heading over `components/shared/Contest`, and that widget is the home page's Wager Contest tab.** They are the same component from the same chunk at the same size — verified, not assumed: measured off the page capture at dpr 1.25 the cards come out 2:1:1 on a 12px gap filling the 1216px container and 152px tall (which is `py-7` around a 24px title, `mb-3` and an `h-15` box, exactly), plain rows 40px (`py-3` around `text-xs`) and podium rows 48.5px (the same padding around a `w-5` medal at its own 1.223 aspect). Nothing about the page version is larger; it just sits in a wider container. `.table` moved from `LatestBets.css` to `Contest.css` with it.

**Two fixtures, one contest.** `pages/Home/LatestBets/contest.js` and `pages/DailyContest/contest.js` are the same contest captured on different days under different display currencies — 8/2–8/3 in USD with nine rows, 8/10–8/11 in BCD with ten. Merging them would mean inventing one, so `services/contest.js` exposes `getHomeContest` and `getDailyContest` and both collapse into one call when a real endpoint exists.

**THE ENDPOINTS ARE REAL NOW, AND THE MISTAKE THAT HID THEM IS WORTH REPEATING BACK.** This section used to say `ENDPOINTS.dailyContest` was invented because "the chunk was recoverable but a chunk carries markup, not requests". That is true of a chunk you read for its templates and false of one you read to the top: `Contest-Uqb1GQAo.js` opens with its own request module, `G = {api_info, api_list, api_rank, api_prev}`, and spells all four paths out. **Re-read a recovered chunk for its API namespace before inventing a path** — the same trick that gave `/deposit-offer` its two, one page over.

**Three of the four answer unauthenticated and the fourth does not**, probed on `hash.game`, and where the split falls is the `game/support` rule again: a leaderboard is public, your place in it is not. `infos` returns the ladder (`winnerNumber: 10`, `userBonusRate` [.5 .25 .12 .06 .03 .015 .009 .007 .005 .004]), `<type>/list/` the active standings with the `****` sentinel intact, `<type>/last/` the previous round — which is where Last Champion comes from, and its live answer names `JabyBaby15`, the same player the signed-in reference capture shows. `<type>/rank/` is 6002. `<type>` is `daily` | `weekly` | `monthly`; only daily is built.

**The ladder is now a field rather than a reading of the rows.** `userBonusRate` went into both fixtures verbatim off the live `infos` response, and its ten fractions are the same ten each standing already carried as its own `rate` — two independent sources agreeing, one read off a rendered page and one fetched without reference to it. It is kept apart from the rows because the ORIGINAL keeps it apart, and the signed-in row's "To reach Top N" counts the LADDER and not the players on the board.

That currency split is what the widget's `money`/`poolMoney` reproduce — a fiat display currency gets `$` and a fixed 2dp, a crypto gets its ticker after the figure and trailing zeros trimmed (`91,735,608 BCD` beside `2,253,306.2 BCD`; ragged precision is correct, not a rounding slip). The prize **pool** takes 2dp either way.

Hidden players use the site's own `****` sentinel from `context/account.js` — and a hidden row is not merely a different string, it renders a step dimmer in `quarterary` (sampled at #A1ABAF against a named player's pure white). The countdown is **static on purpose**: the payload carries a remaining h/m/s rather than an end instant, and there is no "1st of the month, 00:00 UTC" rule to derive one from the way there is on the deposit-bonus page. Ticking a captured number down to zero and sticking there reads worse than a clock that does not move.

### Signing in

Two things change, and only one of them is in the chunk.

**A MY POSITION ROW APPEARS, AND IT BELONGS TO THE WIDGET RATHER THAN THE PAGE.** `Jt`, rendered by `Gt` as `<Show when={k.login}><Jt/></Show>` between Last Champion and the standings table — so it lands on the home page's Wager Contest tab off the same condition, and neither call site passes anything. **Both arms are built and they are different trees, not one responsive one**: the phone is a name block with the "to reach" line under it over two divided cells (`kt`), the desktop is four equal cells in a row with that line as the fourth (`Ht`), so `useIsDesktop()` branches. The extra wrapper div around the desktop's "My Position" cell — a bare `flex-1 border-l` holding a `center flex-1 flex-col` where its two neighbours are that class list directly — is the chunk's and is kept.

**Nothing in it is invented, because the shut endpoint's fixture is the store's own declared default** — the fourth time checking for one has paid, after `/bonus`, the referral dashboard and My Profile. The chunk's store opens `{… rank: 0, wager: 0}` and overwrites the pair only once `api_rank` resolves behind a login test, and zero/zero is exactly what the reference renders: "50th+" over "$0.00", the whole point of the row being the distance left. `ve` clamps both ends (`rank == 0 || rank > 50` both print "50th+"), and **`t()`'s out-of-range guard fires on the home fixture**: `activeList.length > t() - 1`, so nine captured rows against a ten-rung ladder yield no target and the home tab reads "To reach Top 10" beside "$0.00". That is a shortfall in that capture, not a rendering choice — the daily fixture has all ten and prints the real gap.

**The pin's `/40` had to be dropped — the `w-30` trap, one more time.** The chunk writes it `bg-[#F6519666]/40`: an eight-digit hex that already encodes 40% alpha, inside a `/40` opacity utility. On Tailwind v3 the modifier wins outright, and the capture proves it — the pill samples rgb(128,65,94) over layer4's #323738, which solves to α = 0.398 / 0.385 / 0.404 across the three channels, a flat 0.4 of #F65196. **Tailwind v4 compiles the pair through `color-mix` and COMPOUNDS them**, emitting `oklab(…/.16)` — checked in the built CSS, not assumed — which all but erases the pin. The bare hex is the same 0.4 by construction and is what ships, with the chunk's line quoted at the call site.

**THE HEADING GOES, AND THE CHUNK DOES NOT SAY WHY.** `tr` gates it on `!q.mobile && "/promotions/daily-contest" === location.pathname` with no reference to the session at all. What is suggestive is that the pathname is read ONCE, imperatively, at component creation rather than through a router hook — so a page created while some other path is current evaluates against that path and never draws the heading, and `/login/signin` is a dialog route over exactly this page. That is the only mechanism in the chunk that could produce the observed behaviour and it cannot be confirmed from here. It is reproduced against `session.login` because that is what the live page does; the line to change is in `pages/DailyContest/index.jsx` if the stale pathname turns out to be the real cause.

What filled the gap before any of that was recovered is still the useful part, and all three tricks generalise:

- **The i18n bundle outlives the chunk.** Every string on the page — headings, card lines, all four rule bodies, all five FAQ answers — is a verbatim en-US value out of `index-Dn9m_cUp.js`, which *is* saved. **Check it before transcribing prose off a screenshot.** Only two things are inferred, both flagged in `raffle.js`: how the rule sentences group under their four headings (the bundle is a flat alphabetical map and no capture shows an open accordion), and `PRIZE_LADDER`, derived from the winner list — page 1's twenty rows happen to cover every tier.
- **Artwork can be lifted from the captures.** The ticket badge is its per-row silhouette **repainted** with the horizontal gradient it carries, which puts the digits back into text. The hero is the full 1216×264 band, and the region under the card had to be reconstructed — see the next paragraph, because the method is the whole point.
- **The avatar CDN is reachable even though bc.game's origin is not.** `img2.distributedresourcestorage.com/avatar/<id>/s` served all 47 avatars the markup names. They arrive at 320–500px and ~70KB each and collapse to **thirteen distinct images** — most accounts keep a default — so `avatars.js` maps id → file and the directory is 149KB instead of 3.3MB. Two arrived as JPEG rather than PNG; the Node decoder here only does PNG, so those went through `System.Drawing` in PowerShell.

**THE HERO BAND IS THE DEPLOY'S OWN ARTWORK NOW, AND THERE ARE TWO OF THEM.** `mt = () => H(G(gt,ft), G(dt,ut))` in the recovered chunk, where `H(t,e)` is `ee.mobile ? t : e` and `G(t,e)` is `Ce.darken ? t : e` — [mobile, desktop] over [dark, light], with both themes naming the same file in each pair, so the theme half is inert and only the DEVICE half decides. `bg-D_XtxMnW.png` is 796x520-ish portrait art for the phone's `max-sm:h-80` box and `bg-BlfpsWpD.png` is 2400x520, aspect 4.6, which is `sm:aspect-[4.6]` exactly. This build had only the wide one and cropped it with `bg-cover` on a phone, which is three and a half times too wide — the middle of the artwork blown up and cut off. Both are fetched now, and the desktop one REPLACES the reconstruction described below: 2400x520 against 1520x330, with nothing behind the card guessed.

**The two "How to Earn Ticket" illustrations were the deploy's as well** (`ticket-D3xnehT2.png`, `ticket_more-gz_7gilc.png`), and their aspect is why that card read wrong: cropped from the capture they were 188x140 (1.343) against the real 260x229 (1.135), so at the card's `h-28` the artwork came out 138px wide instead of 117 and pushed the text column 21px left. With the real files plus the chunk's own `px-7` and `ml-5` — this build had `px-6.25` and no margin — the text starts 161px into the card, which is the reference to the pixel, and the first card's line wraps after "Log in & Wager" because the column is finally the right width. That line was briefly pinned with `whitespace-nowrap` and must not be: the wrap IS the layout.

The paragraph that follows is kept because the method is the transferable part, not because the file still needs it.

**Reconstructing behind a translucent element is a fit, not an interpolation.** The hero card is 20% black over the artwork, so about four fifths of whatever sits behind it is visible and any reconstruction is on show rather than hidden. Filling that rectangle by interpolating each row between its neighbours — fine under the opaque card an earlier pass assumed — reads as an obvious smear once the real card goes over it. What works: divide the capture's own pixels inside the card by 0.8 to undo the black, drop everything that is card UI rather than artwork, then average the ~80% that survives on a 12px grid and resample. The background there is the tail of a radial glow, smooth and low amplitude, so a coarse fit reproduces it and carries none of the UI's edges through. Un-matting alone is not enough — filling the UI's holes per row leaves horizontal ghosts of the text that was in them.

**The page was built from screenshots first and rewritten from rendered markup later, and the diff is instructive** — the same lesson as `/deposit-offer`, one level up. Geometry survived: every measured box, the 64px accordion pitch, the 1216 container, `basis-56` on the heading (the exact 224px "Game ID" offset). What did **not** survive was *mechanism*:

- The hero card is `bg-black_alpha20` + `backdrop-blur-sm`, not an opaque fill. Solving the samples had produced `alw_dark` at 60% — visually the same place, the wrong mechanism, and it would have broken the moment the artwork behind it changed.
- The jackpot's gold is a **conic** gradient. A vertical sample down a digit stroke cannot tell conic from linear; the reconstruction got the middle of the glyph right and the corners wrong.
- "SUPER LUCKY DRAW" is `absolute -top-4`, overhanging the card — which is why its ink measured as if the card had no top padding.
- The winner table is `flex justify-between` with per-column widths, not the grid the column measurements suggested.
- The header carries a **thirty-entry vertical ticker** on keen-slider in a 16px window, not the single static line the one visible row implied.

Two of the original's own dead classes are kept rather than tidied: `bas` on the heading does nothing, and **`texxt-sm` on the table wrapper is a typo that is load-bearing by accident** — the class does not exist, so the rows inherit the body's 14px instead of being set to it. "Fixing" either would invent a change the live page does not have.

**The schedule is computed, not pinned**, as the deposit bonus's month reset is. The page states its rule ("Weekly raffle draws every Monday at 12:00:00 UTC") and the capture agrees to the second — header `20260817120000` and picker `20260810120000` are the Mondays either side of the capture date, and the printed `6d:2h:37m:19s` is the gap to the first. `drawId`/`drawInstant` in `services/raffle.js` are the two halves.

**My Tickets — the signed-in tab, and its fixture is the STORE'S OWN DECLARED STATE.** `Je` in the chunk is the initial literal, and every figure the pane prints comes out of it: `statistics` all zero, both ticket lists `{page:1, pageSize:20, total:0, totalPage:0, list:[]}`, `periodTicketCount: 0`, `round`/`winningRound` 0. That is the fourth time checking for one of these has supplied a login-gated fixture, after `/bonus`, the referral dashboard and My Profile — and here it lands exactly on the phone capture of a fresh account: three zeros over the site's empty placeholder. The outer strip is TWO tabs with the first login-gated (`<Show when={K.login}><TabItem title="My Tickets"/></Show><TabItem title="Results"/>`), so index 0 is My Tickets signed in and Results signed out, which is how one strip serves both states without renumbering. Inside it: the statistics card — drawn as a torn ticket stub, a dashed rule with two `bg-layer4` discs punching notches in it, all four flipping axis at `lg` — over an `indicator` strip of Active / Past / My Winnings. The "$100.00" the Active pane asks you to wager is `He(progress)` computed, not a literal: `Decimal(dayUse ? 1000 : 100)` scaled by the remaining fraction of the goal, which at `goals: 0` is the target itself. Not built: the draw pickers on Past and My Winnings (each gated on its own round being set, which nothing here sets) and the pager under a list that is always empty.

**Only page 1 of the winner list exists.** The pager reads "01 of 08" because that is the draw's own figure; pages 2–8 and every other draw in the picker render the site's empty state rather than invented winners.

Only desktop was captured, so nothing below `lg` has been seen. `components/shared/Pagination` gained an `align` prop for this page (centre stays the default), and `.rounded-lg10` — the original's own 10px radius, which Tailwind has no step for — went into `index.css`.

Are You A Compulsive Gambler?

According to Gamblers Anonymous, answering 'yes' to seven or more of the following questions suggests a gambling addiction, for which help should be sought:

  • Has gambling made you lose significant time from work or school?
  • Has it negatively impacted your home life?
  • Has it damaged your reputation?
  • Have you felt remorse after gambling?
  • Do you gamble to solve financial issues?
  • Has gambling diminished your ambition or efficiency?
  • Do you continue gambling to win back losses?
  • After winning, do you feel a strong urge to return and win more?
  • Do you often gamble until your last dollar is spent?
  • Do you borrow money to finance gambling?
  • Have you sold possessions to fund gambling?
  • Are you reluctant to spend "gambling money" on normal expenditures?
  • Does gambling make you careless of your own or your family's welfare?
  • Do you gamble for longer than intended?
  • Do you gamble to escape worries?
  • Have you committed or considered committing illegal acts to finance gambling?
  • Does gambling disrupt your sleep?
  • Do you gamble when emotionally upset?
  • Do you gamble to celebrate good fortune?
  • Have you considered self-harm or suicide due to gambling?

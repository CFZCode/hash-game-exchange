Overview

To thank our players for their continued support, BC is launching a time-limited 100% RTP event for Original Games provided by BC Originals. This promotion is backed by a 1 billion BC Token reward pool and allows eligible players to experience Original Games at 100% RTP during the promotional period.

RTP (Return to Player) represents the percentage of total wagers that a game is designed to return to players over time. For example, a game with a 97% RTP is expected to return $97 for every $100 wagered on average. During this event, eligible Original Games provided by BC Originals operate at 100% RTP, meaning that the house edge is offset through instant rakeback within the applicable allowance.

When you are within your Zero Edge allowance, bets placed on eligible Original Games receive instant rakeback calculated dynamically based on the actual house edge.

Key Features

  • 100% RTP on Original Games provided by BC Originals (within allowance)
  • Zero Edge mechanism achieved through instant rakeback
  • Limited-time promotion, subject to reward pool availability

Rules

  • Eligible Games: Original Games provided by BC Originals
  • Prize Pool: 1 billion BC Tokens
  • Daily Limit: The allowance increases with gameplay and automatically resets daily at 00:00 (UTC)
  • Settlement Method: Instant rakeback (Tacoback) paid in BC Tokens

Additional Information

  • Tacoback rewards are converted and credited based on the best available exchange rate in real time.
  • The reward pool is limited and distributed on a first-come, first-served basis. If the final reward payout exceeds the remaining balance of the pool, the payout will be capped at the remaining amount.
  • As 100% RTP is already applied to Original Games within the allowance, eligible wagers will not be counted toward other bonuses ( including level up xp ), promotions, or affiliate wagering calculations.
